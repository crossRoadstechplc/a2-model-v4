A2 Charging & Platform Model - Replication Reference Guide
==========================================================


Purpose
-------
This file is the implementation reference for rebuilding the workbook
`A2 E FLEET OPERATIONS  FINANCIALS-VER 2.xlsx` exactly as it behaves today so that:
1. loading the current Excel inputs reproduces the current Excel outputs exactly, and
2. changing any workbook-backed input recalculates all dependent results recursively.

Important scope note
--------------------
This workbook is not a set of three standalone financial statements for
A2 Energy, A2 Platform, and A2 Fleet.

It is a combined charging / battery swapping / platform model whose
commercial demand is driven by fleet deployment assumptions.

Interpret the workbook as:
- Fleet demand drivers live mainly in `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA` and `POWER CALCULATIONS`
- Energy economics live mainly in power sales and power purchase lines
- Platform economics live mainly in subscription revenue and software capex
- Financial statements are combined at workbook level, not split into three entity-ledger statements

Source file
-----------
- Workbook: `A2 E FLEET OPERATIONS  FINANCIALS-VER 2.xlsx`

Core modeling principle
-----------------------
Treat the workbook as the source of truth for:
- sheet names
- row labels
- cell locations
- literal formulas
- hardcoded constants
- baseline output values

Do not normalize, rename, or "fix" formulas during the first replication pass.
Replicate literal workbook behavior first, including quirks and label mismatches.

Workbook flow
-------------
ASSUMPTIONS_DATA
  -> POWER CALCULATIONS
  -> REVENUE PROJECTION
  -> CAPEX & DEPRECIATION
  -> SOURCE_USE OF FUNDS
  -> INCOME STATEMENT 
  -> CASH FLOW
  -> BALANCE SHEET
  -> VALUATION SUMMARY
  -> KEY METRICS

Sheet roles
-----------
- `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA`
  Master driver sheet for network build, truck deployment, swap operations,
  battery inventory, infrastructure costs, software / hardware cost, reserves,
  and total investment.

- `POWER CALCULATIONS`
  Fleet energy-demand and charging-load sheet.
  Converts truck deployment into annual power units consumed and cost of power.

- `REVENUE PROJECTION`
  Commercial revenue layer.
  Builds revenue from power sales plus software subscriptions.

- `CAPEX & DEPRECIATION`
  Capex schedule, depreciation schedule, total depreciation,
  and asset value roll-forwards.

- `SOURCE_USE OF FUNDS`
  Equity funding and use-of-funds schedule for the combined workbook case.

- `INCOME STATEMENT `
  Combined operating statement for energy sales plus platform revenue.

- `CASH FLOW`
  Combined cash flow statement.

- `BALANCE SHEET`
  Combined balance sheet with retained earnings plug behavior.

- `VALUATION SUMMARY`
  EBITDA multiple valuation, investor stake value, MOIC, and reference IRR linkage.

- `KEY METRICS`
  EBITDA margin, net margin, and ROE.

Important derived cascades
--------------------------
1. Fleet deployment cascade
- `NUMBER OF TRUCKS ADDED` -> `NUMBER OF TRUCKS CUMALATIVE`
- cumulative trucks -> total swaps per day

2. Charging infrastructure cascade
- swaps per day -> battery packs to swap per day
- battery packs to swap per day -> chargers required
- chargers required -> chargers per station
- swap throughput -> swapping bays per station

3. Battery inventory cascade
- trucks added x packs per truck -> battery packs required
- replacements + additions -> total number of battery packs

4. Energy demand cascade
- trucks -> fleet kilometres
- battery parameters -> energy used per km
- kilometres x energy intensity -> total power units consumed per year
- power units x purchase rate -> cost of power

5. Revenue cascade
- power units sold x sale price per unit -> revenue from power sales
- users x subscription charge -> revenue from subscriptions
- combined revenue feeds the income statement

6. Capex cascade
- stations + chargers + swapping bays + software + hardware
  -> total capex
- reserves and contingencies remain in assumptions / sources and uses

7. Funding cascade
- total investment less truck and battery-pack capex lines
  -> investor subscription / use-of-funds schedule

8. Financial statement cascade
- revenue + power cost + opex + lease charges + depreciation
  -> EBITDA -> EBT -> tax -> EAT
- EAT + depreciation + capex + equity injection
  -> cash flow
- cash + asset values + retained earnings plug
  -> balance sheet
- EBITDA multiple valuation + investor 25% stake value
  -> MOIC / IRR reference

Critical workbook quirks to preserve
------------------------------------
1. The sheet name `INCOME STATEMENT ` has a trailing space.
2. `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA!J16` is hardcoded to `103` while surrounding years are formula-driven.
3. `SOURCE_USE OF FUNDS!C5:M5` is labeled `EQUITY-INVESTOR SUBSCRIPTION (25%)`
   but does not behave as a simple 25% formula.
   It is effectively:
   `total investment - truck capex - battery pack capex`
   with `C5` also adding contingency reserve.
4. `SOURCE_USE OF FUNDS!A14` is itself a formula reference to `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA!A36`
   and resolves to the operating reserve label.
5. `INCOME STATEMENT !N10 = M10*1.2` instead of a direct `N3*0.06` style formula.
6. `INCOME STATEMENT !N15 = M15*1.2` instead of a direct `N14*0.3`.
7. `CAPEX & DEPRECIATION!P6` links to `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA!N33`
   and `P7` links to `ASSUMPTIONS_DATA!N34`.
   These appear misaligned but currently evaluate to zero; preserve literal links first.
8. `CAPEX & DEPRECIATION` contains a row labeled `BATTERY PACKS` in asset values,
   but that roll-forward is built from the swapping-bays depreciation block.
   Preserve literal workbook behavior before reclassifying.
9. `CAPEX & DEPRECIATION!E11:P11` (`CAPEX - CLOSING BALANCE`) goes negative in later years.
   Do not treat it as a cleaned book-value schedule unless formulas are intentionally reworked later.
10. Balance-sheet retained earnings behaves as a plug:
    total assets - equity = retained earnings.
11. The workbook uses direct cell links extensively.
    Row positions and exact cell references matter.
